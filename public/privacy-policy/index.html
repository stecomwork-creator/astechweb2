We are the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@astechglobal.ltd.

Data we handle

  • Contact details and messages. When you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive your name and contact details, your message, and anything else you choose to share.

Our website has no contact form, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use personal data

  • To reply to your enquiries and to discuss or provide our services.

  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

Social media

We publish content to our own business accounts, including our LinkedIn company page, using an internal publishing tool. A member of our team reviews each post before it is published. Post drafts and images may be created with the help of an AI service, using the topic and details our team provides.

The tool uses platform APIs only to publish our content and manage our own accounts. It does not collect or store the personal data of platform members. If you interact with our accounts, the platform you are using also handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Service providers

We share personal data only as needed with providers that help us run our business:

  • Vercel hosts our website.
  • Google provides our business email and the fonts on our website.
  • WhatsApp and LinkedIn handle your data when you follow our links to them or contact us there.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

Cookies

Our website code does not set cookies. Third-party sites that you visit through our links may use their own.

How long we keep data

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or for as long as the law requires.
